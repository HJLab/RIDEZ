RIDEZ Indstillinger v72

- Collapsed settings show only Lyde and Telefon & sensorer.
- Lyde opens the single Lyde i RIDEZ on/off control.
- Telefon & sensorer opens the phone calibration and live sensor area.
- Telefon & sensorer is green when calibration is valid.
- If calibration is missing or fails, both Telefon & sensorer and Indstillinger are red and blinking.
- No Supabase changes are required.
